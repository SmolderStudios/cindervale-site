CATEGORIES

Two secret rules. One grid of anime. Read them before they read you.

Play solo

Take on the machine. It picks a secret category, answers you straight, and works on yours the whole time.

Play a friend

Make a room and send the link. They join from anywhere — phone, laptop, whatever's nearby.
How to play
  1. You each take a secret category — "from One Piece", "fights with a sword", "is royalty". Your opponent never sees it.
  2. On your turn you probe: send over any anime character. Your opponent answers yes or no — does that character fit their category?
  3. Answers sort into FITS and DOESN'T. That's your evidence board.
  4. Instead of probing, spend a turn to call it and name their category.
  5. Call it right and you win. Call it wrong and you've burned your turn. First correct call takes it.
