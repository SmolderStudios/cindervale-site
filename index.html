Ten guilds

Somebody always
wants something doing

One guild for every skill, plus the Nightmarket. Join and the board hands you work: kill these, gather that, bring back this many.

  • Three jobs a day, small to long, rerolled every morning.
  • Titles that do something. Six ranks per guild, each a real bonus in that guild's own skill. Speed, yield, gold, a shot at double output.
  • A shop each, paid for in tokens off the board.
  • One skip, for the day the board asks for something absurd.
Thirteen skills

Everything feeds
something else

What you cut down becomes what you build. What you build decides how deep you get. What you drag back out of the dark is what the best tools are made of.

  • A 98-point tree behind every skill, and 98 points is exactly enough to fill one. Getting there is the long part: points arrive a level at a time, and the Grandmaster tier stays shut until every base node under it is maxed.
  • Tools in tiers, with the top ones gated behind drops you have to go and fight for.
  • 735 items, every one logged the first time you see it.
Twelve zones

Down as far as the
Demon Sanctum

Every zone has its own monsters, a boss at the end of it, and one rare spawn worth waiting for. Fifty-seven in all, each logged with what it drops and how often.

  • Four raids with no retreat. Clear every stage or leave with nothing.
  • Gems and 52 enchants to put into what you are wearing.
  • A 37-node mastery tree on top of everything else.
  • Slayer bounties from three masters, and points to spend on perks.
Twenty-six islands

Send it out and
go do something else

Build a ship, sign a crew, pick a heading. Voyages run on a real clock and come home whether the game is open or not.

  • Seven hulls, raft up to leviathan, with cannons, rudders and figureheads to bolt onto them.
  • Four seas, and a chart that fills itself in as you go.
  • A consort who will sail with you once you are far enough out.

About

Made by one person

Cindervale Idle is the work of a solo developer who likes the tick of a good incremental game and the weight of dark fantasy, and wanted one that was both.

It is a single hand-built game, bought once. No microtransactions and no pay-to-win — the only thing that gets you further is playing. Every monster, item and zone has its own hand-drawn icon.

It is in Early Access and gets updated constantly. Still to come: magic and ranged combat, more zones, and more of everything already here. Feedback goes straight to the developer.
