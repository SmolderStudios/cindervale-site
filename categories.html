How to play
  1. You each invent a secret category and type it in — "has white hair", "is a villain", "carries a sword", "from a sports anime". Your opponent never sees it.
  2. On your turn you probe: pick any anime character and send them over. Your opponent answers yes or no — does that character fit their category?
  3. Nearly 1,500 characters, so use search and the Tags filter to hunt down a probe — stack tags like FIRE + MAIN CHARACTER to test a hunch in one go.
  4. Every answer you collect sorts into two columns: FITS and DOESN'T. That's your evidence board for cracking their rule.
  5. Instead of probing, you can spend your turn to call it — type out what you think their category is. They rule on whether you nailed it.
  6. Guess right and you win. Guess wrong and you've burned your turn. First correct call takes it.
