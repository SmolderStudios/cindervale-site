One of you sees the target. The other has one clue to find it.

Same device

Sat next to each other? Pass the phone back and forth — the screen covers itself between turns so nobody sees the target.
How to play
  1. Each round you get a spectrum — two opposites, like Cold ←→ Hot, and a dial between them.
  2. One of you is the psychic. You alone see a hidden target sitting somewhere on that dial.
  3. The psychic gives one clue: anything in the world that sits exactly there on the spectrum. Not "warm" — say a cup of tea.
  4. The other player spins the needle to where they think that clue lands, and locks it in.
  5. Scoring: 4 dead centre, 3 close, 2 on the edge of the target, 0 outside it.
  6. You're on the same team — the score is shared. Roles swap every round. Chase a high average.
  7. Dealt a spectrum you can't work with? The psychic can swap the card — three swaps per game.
