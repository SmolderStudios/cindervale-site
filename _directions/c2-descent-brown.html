Ten guilds

Somebody always
wants something doing

One guild for every skill, plus the Nightmarket. Join and the board hands you work: kill these, gather that, bring back this many.

  • Three jobs a day, small to long, rerolled every morning.
  • Titles that do something. Six ranks, each a real bonus in that guild's skill.
  • One skip, for the day the board asks for something absurd.
Thirteen skills

Everything feeds
something else

What you cut down becomes what you build. What you build decides how deep you get. What you drag back out of the dark is what the best tools are made of.

  • A 98-point tree behind every skill, and never enough points for all of it.
  • Tools in tiers, with the top ones gated behind drops you have to fight for.
  • 735 items, all logged the first time you see one.
Twelve zones

Down as far as the
Demon Sanctum

Each zone has its own monsters, a boss at the end of it, and one rare spawn worth waiting for. Fifty-seven in all, every one logged with what it drops.

  • Four raids with no retreat. Clear every stage or leave with nothing.
  • Gems and 52 enchants to put into what you are wearing.
  • Slayer bounties from three masters, and points to spend on perks.
